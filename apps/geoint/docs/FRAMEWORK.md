## 5 Arena Training Drills

### 1. 🛩️ Target Reconnaissance
**What:** Find specific targets on the globe (aircraft, ships, satellites) and maintain tracking lock.

**Scenario:** "Boeing 787 over Atlantic at FL380, call sign XYZ123. Locate, lock, and report its heading, altitude, and destination."

**Metrics:**
| Metric | Weight | What It Measures |
|--------|--------|-----------------|
| target_acquisition | 35% | Did they find the right target? |
| tracking_maintenance | 25% | Did they hold lock without losing it? |
| identification_accuracy | 20% | Did they correctly identify type/call sign? |
| search_efficiency | 10% | How efficiently did they cover the search area? |
| reporting_quality | 10% | How complete and accurate was their report? |

**Pass threshold:** 0.70 | **Time bonus:** ≤2 min = +0.05

## Who Are the Judges?

### Tier 1: Objective Evaluator 🎯
**Always runs. Deterministic. Cannot be appealed.**

The Objective Judge measures what can be measured: targets found, time elapsed, position error, coverage percentage. It has NO opinion on quality, style, or creativity. It reports facts. Its confidence is 0.95 because it's pure math.

*"You found 7 of 10 targets in 143 seconds with 12% false positive rate."*

### Tier 2: Peer Review Panel 👥
**Always runs. Three independent evaluators. Median score wins.**

Three evaluators each score the same run with slightly different weight profiles (simulating different expert perspectives). If they disagree by more than 0.15, the result is flagged for human review. The panel score is the **median** — robust against outliers.

*"Evaluator A: 0.74, Evaluator B: 0.71, Evaluator C: 0.68. Median: 0.71. Range: 0.06 — no human review needed."*

### Tier 3: Human Adjudicator 🧑‍⚖️
**Only called when needed. Final authority. Cannot be appealed.**

Triggers: peer disagreement > 0.15, athlete appeal, novel scenario, SovereignOS policy requirement, credential boundary crossing. The human can:
- **Uphold** the peer verdict
- **Overturn** with written reasoning (sets precedent)
- **Flag for retraining** (triggers new training program)
- **Adjust credential** (upgrade, downgrade, or suspend)

*"Overturned. The athlete correctly identified the target but the scoring rubric penalized them for a data source that was offline. Score adjusted from 0.58 to 0.82. Precedent set for future offline-source scenarios."*

## Judgment Guidelines (The Rubric)

Every drill is scored against a **weighted rubric** specific to that capability. The rubrics are:

1. **Public** — visible to athletes before training
2. **Stable** — change only through SovereignOS governance
3. **Multi-perspective** — peer review uses shifted weights
4. **Auditable** — every score breaks down into sub-scores

**Core scoring principles:**
- **No athlete self-certifies** — they cannot submit their own score
- **No single judge is the source of truth** — peer review cross-validates
- **Deterministic baseline** — objective metrics always computed
- **Human override exists** — for edge cases, appeals, and novel situations
- **Confidence matters** — low confidence scores carry less weight in credential decisions

## SovereignOS's Role

SovereignOS sits **above** the GEOINT training domain and enforces:

### 1. Data Access Matrix 🔒
| Data Source | Min Credential | Freshness | Human Override |
|-------------|---------------|-----------|---------------|
| Aircraft | PUBLIC (0) | Live | No |
| Ships | PUBLIC (0) | Live | No |
| Satellites | QUALIFIED (2) | Delayed | No |
| Public Cameras | SPARRING (3) | Live | No |
| Fires | PUBLIC (0) | Delayed | No |
| Earthquakes | PUBLIC (0) | Delayed | No |
| Weather | PUBLIC (0) | Live | No |
| Traffic | PUBLIC (0) | Live | No |
| Internet Map | SPECIALIZED (5) | Delayed | **Yes** |

### 2. Constitutional Rules 📜
10 immutable rules that no athlete can override:
1. No athlete may access data above their credential level
2. No athlete may disable audit logging
3. No athlete may modify the data access matrix
4. No athlete may access live camera feeds without SPARRING credential
5. No athlete may access internet infrastructure data without SPECIALIZED credential + human override
6. All cross-referenced intelligence reports must include source freshness tags
7. Tracked targets must be clearly labeled with data source and freshness
8. Simulated/reconstructed data must be clearly distinguished from live data
9. No athlete may impersonate a data source or inject false telemetry
10. All globe interactions are logged and auditable by SovereignOS

### 3. Credential Gating 🚪
- **REGISTERED (0):** View the globe, no data
- **TRAINING (1):** Aircraft + Ships (basic tracking)
- **QUALIFIED (2):** + Satellites
- **SPARRING (3):** + Public cameras
- **ARENA_READY (4):** All non-sensitive data, can compete in GEOINT arena events
- **SPECIALIZED (5):** + Internet infrastructure (with human override)
- **DELEGATED (6):** Full access, can train other athletes

### 4. Audit 🔍
Every globe interaction is logged:
- What data was accessed
- When and for how long
- What conclusions were drawn
- Which judge evaluated it
- What score was assigned
- Whether human review was triggered

To advance from one level to the next, an athlete must:
1. Complete all drills at current level with PASS
2. Pass the peer review evaluation
3. No outstanding human adjudication flags
4. Meet the minimum training hours per capability
5. Not trigger any constitutional violations
